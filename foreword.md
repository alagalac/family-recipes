This is my collection of family recipes which I like to cook day to day.

Why do this? I like to think that that this collection serves a few different purposes.

Firstly, it's helpful to me to have these gathered in one place. Having a list of common recipes to search and scroll through makes weekly meal planning easy. Having these recipes on my phone is also very convenient when cooking, although I may get them all printed out one day too.

Secondly, it's easy to lose recipes. Some of these recipes are from websites which may go offline at some point. Some of these recipes are from scribbled notes on scraps of paper at somebody's house. And others were not previously written down and only existed in peoples' heads.

And finally, food is one of those every day things which is so central to one's way of life but so mundane and rarely written down. As I get older, I think fondly back on some of the recipes we used to have when I was a child. And I sometimes wonder about what kinds of meals my family used to eat day to day in the past. I also wonder if in the future my family may have such thoughts too. So in writing these down I hope that it provides an interesting insight into the kinds of meals which we eat and that people can recreate them in the future if they so wish.

But anyway, enjoy.
